What is Flowlix?

Automation that thinks, not just executes.

Flowlix aims to combine traditional workflow automation with AI agents, allowing workflows to understand tasks, make decisions, interact with tools and APIs, and execute actions automatically.

✦

AI-Powered

Bring AI agents into your workflows so they can reason, decide, and help execute complex tasks.

◇

Visual Workflows

Design automation as connected building blocks instead of managing complicated scripts manually.

Open Source

Flowlix is being developed openly with the goal of making powerful AI automation accessible to everyone.

The idea

Connect intelligence to action.

Create workflows where AI can understand a task, choose what to do next, interact with tools and APIs, and continue until the objective is completed.

Flowlix workflow
Trigger Start workflow
AI Agent Understand task
Tools & APIs Take action
Result Complete task
Vision

Make powerful AI automation accessible.

The long-term vision of Flowlix is to make intelligent automation easier for developers and everyday users — without requiring everyone to become an automation expert.
